Who we are

About Us

The CSI-SAU Student Chapter is South Asian University's dynamic hub for technology and innovation. We are a passionate community of student developers, coders, and creators dedicated to building a vibrant tech culture.

We connect our members with the future of computer science by hosting workshops, coding competitions, and guest lectures from industry leaders.

Officially affiliated with the Computer Society of India
