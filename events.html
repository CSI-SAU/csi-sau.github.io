CSI SAU Highlights

Big ideas, bold execution, and moments worth revisiting.

From ideation challenges and coding battles to symposium conversations, our flagship events capture the energy of a student community building for the future.

Flagship Event 2025

AavishkarX 2025

AavishkarX brought together teams to pitch fresh, real-world tech solutions through rapid brainstorming, mentoring, and live presentation rounds. The event celebrated experimentation, collaboration, and the confidence to turn ideas into prototypes with impact.

  • Date: November 14-15, 2025
  • Theme: Innovation, ideation, and problem solving
  • Format: Ideathon + presentation sprint

Flagship Event 2026

SyntaX 2026

SyntaX is CSI SAU's annual coding challenge, designed to test speed, precision, and algorithmic thinking through multi-round problem-solving. The competition brings together some of the sharpest problem-solvers on campus to battle through difficult constraints and crisp execution.

  • Date: January 27, 2026
  • Theme: Competitive programming and coding excellence
  • Format: Multi-round challenge

Flagship Event Forum

Tech-Symposium

The Tech-Symposium creates a platform for meaningful conversations at the intersection of technology, research, and career growth. It brings together students, faculty, and technical leaders for sessions that widen perspective and inspire deeper engagement with emerging systems and ideas.

  • Focus: AI, research, systems, and tech careers
  • Format: Talks, panels, and community exchange
  • Experience: A shared space for learning and connection
